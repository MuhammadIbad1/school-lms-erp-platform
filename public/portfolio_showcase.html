Security & Integrity

12. Enterprise Security Middleware Guards

Security Layer
Middleware Guard Alias Purpose & Protection Strategy
EnsureAccountIsActive active Terminates sessions instantly for deactivated or suspended accounts with custom alerts.
RoleMiddleware role:admin,teacher... Enforces role-level route barriers preventing cross-portal unauthorized access.
PermissionMiddleware permission:student.admit... Enforces granular capability checks for specific actions (create, edit, delete, approve).
VerifyParentChildAccess parent.child Prevents IDOR attacks by strictly verifying that requested student records belong to the logged-in guardian.
VerifyStudentSelfAccess student.self Isolates student data boundaries, preventing students from inspecting or accessing peer records.
