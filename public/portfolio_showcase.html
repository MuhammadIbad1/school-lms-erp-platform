Detailed Breakdown of All System Capabilities

Every feature, workflow, algorithm, and module implemented across the entire platform.

1. Authentication & Enterprise RBAC

  • Multi-Role Login Gateway: Session auth with password hashing.
  • 1-Click Demo Switcher: Instant switching between Admin, Teacher, Student, Parent.
  • Dynamic Role Creation: Create custom security roles with custom capability sets.
  • Visual Capability Matrix: 1-click grant/revoke toggles for granular permissions.
  • User Role Assignment: Instant role reassignment from user directory.
  • 5 Custom Security Middlewares: `active`, `role`, `permission`, `parent.child`, `student.self`.

2. Academic Hierarchy & Placements

  • Academic Sessions: Manage school terms and active year scoping.
  • Classes & Sections: Dynamic class definitions with section splits.
  • Curriculum Subjects: Course codes, credit hours, and passing score criteria.
  • Time Slots Engine: Period durations, break intervals, and recess marking.
  • Teacher Allocation Matrix: Assign faculty to specific subjects and sections.

3. Student Admission & 360° Profiles

  • Multi-Step Admission Wizard: Demographics, parent linking, and login account.
  • Auxiliary Allocations: Assign transport fleet routes & hostel dorm rooms.
  • Automatic Invoicing: Auto-generates initial term fee invoice on enrollment.
  • Student Directory: Class filters, search bars, active/suspend account toggles.
  • 360° Student Profile: Live attendance rate, exam marks, fee ledger, and hostel info.

4. Timetable & Daily Attendance

  • Live Schedule Grid: Full weekly timetable with room allocations.
  • 1-Click Batch Attendance: Teachers mark `Present`, `Late`, `Absent`, `Excused`.
  • Instant Mark All Present: Fast 1-click batch button for quick morning roll call.
  • Custom Attendance Remarks: Log absence reasons or late arrival notes.
  • Aggregate Presence Statistics: Live percentage calculation for students & parents.

5. Examinations & Gradebook

  • Exam Terms: Midterms, Finals, and Quizzes with start/end schedules.
  • Score Entry Matrix: Fast score input with max score constraints.
  • Grade Rule Calculation: Real-time evaluation into `A+`, `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`, `F` and GPA.
  • Teacher Feedback: Constructive evaluation notes per subject.
  • Official Report Card: Printable PDF-ready term report card with dean seal.

6. LMS Studio & Student Homework

  • Study Materials Center: Upload lecture handouts, PDFs, and reading notes.
  • Homework Builder: Set title, instructions, specs attachment, and due date.
  • Student Solution Submissions: Upload homework files (PDF, ZIP, DOCX).
  • Grading Desk: Download submitted files, enter scores, and give feedback.
  • Overdue Tracking: Visual deadline indicators for pending student work.

7. Finance, Fees & Dual Payment

  • Fee Category Masters: Tuition, Science Lab, Transport, Sports.
  • 1-Click Batch Invoicing: Invoices entire classes simultaneously.
  • 💳 Online Card Payment: Automated instant settlement with card checkout.
  • 💵 Cash at School Desk: Request cash verification at school counter.
  • HR Cash Approval Queue: Verify physical cash and approve with 1-click.
  • Official Fee Receipts: Printable vouchers with transaction audit trails.

8. Faculty Payroll & Payslips

  • Salary Slip Generator: Monthly payroll calculation for teachers.
  • Allowances & Deductions: Academic allowances and statutory taxes.
  • Net Disbursed Pay: Automated take-home compensation calculation.
  • 🖨️ Printable Payslip Voucher: A4 formatted with institutional seals.
  • 💾 Direct File Download: 1-click export of official salary vouchers.

9. 7 Auxiliaries Suite

  • 📚 Library Catalog: ISBN lookup, quantity stock, and borrowing ledger.
  • 🚌 Transport Fleet: Transit routes, fares, vehicle plates, driver contacts.

Security & Integrity

12. Enterprise Security Middleware Guards

Security Layer
Middleware Guard Alias Purpose & Protection Strategy
EnsureAccountIsActive active Terminates sessions instantly for deactivated or suspended accounts with custom alerts.
RoleMiddleware role:admin,teacher... Enforces role-level route barriers preventing cross-portal unauthorized access.
PermissionMiddleware permission:student.admit... Enforces granular capability checks for specific actions (create, edit, delete, approve).
VerifyParentChildAccess parent.child Prevents IDOR attacks by strictly verifying that requested student records belong to the logged-in guardian.
VerifyStudentSelfAccess student.self Isolates student data boundaries, preventing students from inspecting or accessing peer records.
