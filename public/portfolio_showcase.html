Campus Auxiliaries & Security Architecture

15. Library Catalog, 16. Transport Fleet & Security Guards

📚 15. Library Catalog & Circulation:

🚌 16. Transport Transit Fleet & Routes:

Vehicle plates, transit routes, monthly fares, driver contacts, and assigned student rider rosters.

🛡️ 5 Enterprise Security Middleware Guards & Protection Strategy:

• EnsureAccountIsActive: Instantly terminates sessions for deactivated/suspended accounts.
• RoleMiddleware: Enforces role-level route barriers preventing cross-portal unauthorized access.
• PermissionMiddleware: Enforces granular capability checks for specific actions (create, edit, delete).
• VerifyParentChildAccess: Prevents IDOR attacks by verifying student records belong to the logged guardian.
